# PulseMind Tools

This file is documentation only. The app should use `toolDefinitions.js` as the source of truth.

All tool ids must use snake_case and must match the keys in `toolDefinitions.js`.
Do not use kebab-case ids like `thought-dump`.
Do not duplicate old instructions here if they differ from `toolDefinitions.js`.

## breathing

Name: Breathing
Type: timer
Duration seconds: 60

Instruction:
Take 1 minute. Breathe in slowly, pause for a moment, then breathe out longer than you breathed in. Repeat 5 times.

Sample entry:
I breathed slowly for 1 minute. My body feels a little calmer.

## grounding

Name: Grounding
Type: fields

Instruction:
Look around and notice what you can see, feel, hear, smell, and taste or sense in your body.

Fields:
- I see
- I feel
- I hear
- I smell
- I taste / notice

Sample entry:
I see my desk, screen, cup, wall, and window.
I feel my feet on the floor.
I hear the fan.
I smell coffee.
I taste water.

## thought_dump

Name: Thought Dump
Type: fields

Instruction:
Write down 3 worries without trying to solve them right now.

Fields:
- I worry
- I worry
- I worry

Sample entry:
I worry about deadlines.
I worry about my project.
I worry that I am falling behind.

## over_responsibility

Name: Working with Over-Responsibility
Type: fields

Instruction:
Write one task you feel responsible for, name the reality, and choose what you can refuse.

Fields:
- Task
- Reality
- I refuse
- Because

Sample entry:
Task: Fix everything today.
Reality: This is partly mine. I can do one part today, not everything.
I refuse to take responsibility for everything because this is not fully mine.

## strategic_refusal

Name: Strategic Refusal
Type: fields

Instruction:
Write one unrealistic, outdated, or unachievable task you should not continue doing. Explain why refusing it protects your real priorities.

Fields:
- Unachievable task
- Reality
- I need to refuse

Sample entry:
Unachievable task: I wanted to program, work delivery shifts, and learn Chinese at the same time.
Reality: I cannot realistically do all of that right now.
I need to refuse learning Chinese for now because programming and work are higher priorities.

## prioritization

Name: Prioritization
Type: priority
Rows: 3

Instruction:
Write 3 tasks. Mark each one as urgent or not urgent, and important or not important.

Options:
- Urgent / Not urgent
- Important / Not important

Sample entry:
Task 1: Submit assignment — urgent, important.
Task 2: Clean files — not urgent, not important.
Task 3: Fix login bug — not urgent, important.

## asking_for_support

Name: Asking for Support
Type: fields

Instruction:
Write what you need help with and who you asked.

Fields:
- I need help with
- I asked

Sample entry:
I need help with understanding the project structure.
I asked my teammate to explain the main files.

## future_me_letter

Name: Future Me Letter
Type: textarea

Instruction:
Write 3–5 sentences to your future self about what you are working for and what you should protect today.

Sample entry:
Dear future me, I am working to build a stable life. I do not need to destroy myself to get there. Today I will protect my health and take one real step.

## five_minute_pause

Name: 5-Minute Pause
Type: timer
Duration seconds: 300

Instruction:
Pause for 5 minutes. Do not fix anything. Just notice what you feel in your body and mind.

Sample entry:
I paused for 5 minutes. I feel tension in my chest and pressure in my head, but I do not need to act immediately.

## five_minute_task

Name: 5-Minute Task
Type: timer
Duration seconds: 300

Instruction:
Pick one tiny task you can do in 5 minutes. Stop when the timer ends, even if it is not perfect.

Sample entry:
Task: Open the project and find the broken component.
Result: I found the file. That is enough for now.

## evening_release

Name: Evening Release
Type: fields

Instruction:
Write what you are worried about, what can wait until tomorrow, and what you will do next.

Fields:
- I worry about
- It can wait until tomorrow
- Tomorrow I will

Sample entry:
I worry about finishing the whole project tonight.
It can wait until tomorrow: debugging the whole thing.
Tomorrow I will check one error first.

## letter_to_tomorrow_me

Name: Letter to Tomorrow Me
Type: textarea

Instruction:
Write a short note giving tomorrow’s version of you the next step.

Sample entry:
Tomorrow me, start with the API error. You do not need to fix everything at once. Just check the first failing request.

## tomorrow_list

Name: Tomorrow List
Type: fields

Instruction:
Write one thing you can do tomorrow and one thing you can safely postpone.

Fields:
- Tomorrow I can
- I can safely postpone

Sample entry:
Tomorrow I can finish one assignment section.
I can safely postpone redesigning the whole interface.

## light_mode

Name: Light Mode
Type: fields

Instruction:
Write the full task, then choose the smallest achievable part.

Fields:
- Full task
- Achievable part

Sample entry:
Full task: finish the whole page.
Achievable part: make the page open without crashing.

## body_reset

Name: Body Reset
Type: timer
Duration seconds: 120

Instruction:
Drink water, eat something simple, and move for 2 minutes.

Sample entry:
I drank water, ate a snack, and walked around the room. I feel slightly more awake.

## future_sentence

Name: Future Sentence
Type: fields

Instruction:
Complete the sentence: “Doing this helps my future because…”

Fields:
- Doing this helps my future because

Sample entry:
Doing this helps my future because finishing this project moves me closer to practice and work.

## three_good_things

Name: Three Good Things
Type: fields

Instruction:
Write three small good things. They can be very small.

Fields:
- Good thing
- Good thing
- Good thing

Sample entry:
Good thing 1: I cleaned my desk.
Good thing 2: I went for a walk.
Good thing 3: I asked for help instead of giving up.
