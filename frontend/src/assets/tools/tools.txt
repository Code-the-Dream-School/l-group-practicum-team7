Breathing

Instruction:
Take 1 minute. Breathe in slowly, pause for a moment, then breathe out longer than you breathed in. Repeat 5 times.

Sample entry:
I breathed slowly for 1 minute. My body feels a little calmer.

Grounding

Instruction:
Look around and name 5 things you see, 4 things you feel, 3 sounds you hear, 2 smells, and 1 thing you can taste or notice in your body.

Sample entry:
I see my desk, screen, cup, wall, and window. I feel my feet on the floor. I hear the fan.

Thought Dump

Instruction:
Write down 3 worries without trying to solve them right now.

Sample entry:

I am worried about deadlines.
I am worried about my project.
I am worried I am falling behind.
Working with Over-Responsibility

Instruction:
Write one task you feel responsible for. Then ask: “Is this fully mine, partly mine, or not mine?”

Sample entry:
Task: Fix everything today.
Reality: This is partly mine. I can do one part today, not everything.

Strategic Refusal

Instruction:
Choose one task you can refuse, postpone, simplify, or stop doing today.

Sample entry:
I will not rewrite the whole project today. I will only fix one broken screen.

Prioritization

Instruction:
Write 3 tasks. Mark each one as urgent/not urgent and important/not important.

Sample entry:

Submit assignment — urgent, important.
Clean files — not urgent, less important.
Fix login bug — important, not urgent.
Asking for Support

Instruction:
Write one thing you need help with and one person you could ask.

Sample entry:
I need help understanding the project structure. I can ask my teammate to explain the main files.
